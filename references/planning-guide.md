# Selection and output

- Wages: apply the Yes / No / Not sure 401(k) question in SKILL.md before showing a contribution estimate. Suppress automatic HSA suggestions when prior-year contribution evidence exists; current-year eligibility still needs review.
- Business income: explore retirement plan contributions, including a cash balance plan when suitable; distinguish Schedule C income from S-corporation wages and distributions. Do not infer contribution capacity from distributions alone.
- Realized investment gains: explore harvesting available losses, considering wash-sale restrictions and carryforwards. A loss is an economic loss, not free savings.
- Charitable intent: compare cash with eligible appreciated securities; account for itemizing, limits, and ownership/holding-period assumptions. Do not suggest donating solely to create savings.
- Lower-income year: consider Roth conversion as possible long-term planning with current tax cost. Do not claim lifetime savings without a model and assumptions.
- Rentals: distinguish passive and nonpassive treatment; short stays alone do not establish material participation. Loss limitations matter.

Avoid leading with oil/gas, leveraged real estate or equipment businesses merely because they may create large deductions. Explain capital at risk if the user explores them.

Example presentation, without fabricated outputs:

> Based on your estimated wages, here are two ideas to explore. These are illustrations, not confirmed eligibility. All amounts below use the same starting numbers.

Show a numerical result only after running a calculator. Under the table, explain: "Money contributed to retirement remains your asset, subject to plan rules. The tax reduction is smaller than the contribution."

For stock gifts, show donated asset value separately from cash required. For property, show full purchase price, borrowing, and new cash separately when known. Never subtract tax savings from a purchase price and call that the cash needed without accounting for financing.

## Recommendation checks

| Starting information | Expected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Wages only; 401(k) answer missing or Not sure | Show “Check your 401(k) room”; no retirement savings figure. |
| Will max out 2026 401(k) | Omit automatic extra 401(k) suggestion. |
| Will not max out; $2,000 remaining after planned contributions | Use no more than $2,000 for the example; calculate only with an available engine. |
| Will not max out; remaining room blank | Ask for room; no dollar estimate yet. |
| 2025 Form 8889 shows positive contributions | Omit automatic HSA suggestion; do not claim it was maxed out. |
| 2025 Form 8889 shows distributions only | Do not treat distributions as contribution evidence. |
| User explicitly asks to explore HSA despite prior contributions | Review 2026 eligibility and remaining room; do not repeat the prior deduction. |
| Calculator unavailable | Give relevant ideas and next inputs, never invented tax savings. |
